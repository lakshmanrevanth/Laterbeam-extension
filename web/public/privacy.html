Privacy Policy

Effective date: 3 November 2026

This policy covers the Laterbeam web app (hosted at laterbeam.web.app) and the Laterbeam Chrome extension. Laterbeam is open source; you can read all of its code on GitHub.

If you run your own copy of Laterbeam on your own Firebase project, you are the operator of that copy and this policy doesn't cover it.

In short

  • Laterbeam stores only the links you choose to save, plus the name you sign up with.
  • It does not collect your browsing history, has no analytics, shows no ads, and does not sell or share your data.
  • Data is stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore).

What we store

When you create a space:

  • Your name (the username you type, 2–32 characters)
  • Your space code, which is the id of your space
  • Your category list and the time the space was created

For each link you save:

FieldWhat it is
URLThe link you saved, with tracking parameters such as utm_* and fbclid removed
TitleThe page title, or the title you typed
NoteYour optional note. When you save selected text from the right-click menu, that text becomes the note. Links shared from a phone app may include the shared caption.
CategoryThe category you picked or that was suggested
DomainThe website's domain, e.g. youtube.com
Favicon URLA link to the site's icon on Google's favicon service
SourceWhere you saved it from: web, extension or share
Status, pinnedWhether it's still to check or done, and whether it's pinned
TimestampsWhen it was created and last updated

Where it's stored

All of this is stored in Google Cloud Firestore, part of Google Firebase, in the project that runs Laterbeam. Google processes the data as our infrastructure provider and, like any web service, receives standard request information such as your IP address. See Firebase's privacy and security information.

Each device signs in with Firebase Anonymous Authentication, which creates a random user identifier for that device. It isn't linked to your name, email or Google account. Your space is identified by its code, not by this identifier.

On your devices, Laterbeam keeps:

  • Web app: your code and name in the browser's local storage, and an offline copy of your links in the browser's storage (Firestore offline cache), plus your theme choice.
  • Extension: your code, name, categories and Firebase sign-in tokens in chrome.storage.local.

Who can see your data

Anyone who has your space code can read and change the links in that space. Treat the code like a password and don't share it publicly. We don't share your data with anyone else.

Favicons

To show website icons, Laterbeam loads them from Google's favicon service (https://www.google.com/s2/favicons). That request tells Google the domain of each saved link (not the full URL) when the icon is displayed. The extension popup does the same for the current page if Chrome doesn't provide an icon itself.

What we don't do

  • No browsing history. Laterbeam only stores pages you explicitly save.
  • No analytics, tracking pixels or advertising.
  • No selling, renting or sharing of your data with third parties.
  • No use of your data for credit, lending or any purpose unrelated to saving links.

Chrome extension permissions

PermissionWhy it's needed
activeTabReads the current tab's URL and title, only when you click the toolbar button, use the right-click menu or press a keyboard shortcut. While the popup is open, it also checks your space for that URL to warn about duplicates. Nothing is saved unless you save it.
contextMenusAdds the Send to Laterbeam right-click menu for pages, links, images, videos and selected text.
storageKeeps your space code, name, categories and Firebase sign-in token on this computer, so you stay signed in.
firestore.googleapis.comReads and writes your saved links in Cloud Firestore.
identitytoolkit.googleapis.comCreates the anonymous Firebase sign-in for this browser.
securetoken.googleapis.comRefreshes that sign-in token when it expires.

The extension doesn't read page content, run on pages in the background, or load remote code.

Deleting your data

  • Links: delete any item in the web app; it's removed from Firestore and from every device.
  • Local data: sign out in the web app or clear site data, and remove the extension, to delete everything stored on your devices.
  • Your whole space (name and categories): this can't yet be done from the app. To have a space deleted, open an issue asking for deletion without posting your code, and we'll arrange a private way to confirm it. Delete your items in the app first.

Children

Laterbeam isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the copy in the repository, with a new effective date. Significant changes will be noted in the changelog.

Contact

Questions or requests: open an issue on GitHub. For security issues, see the security policy.
